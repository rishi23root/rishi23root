This is the draft template. It never ships. Copy it, rename the file and `slug` together, replace every section with the real story, and delete the test images. It also doubles as a layout check in `npm run dev:web` at `/writing/draft-template`.

## TL;DR

One or two sentences that answer the title. If someone reads only this, they should leave with the point.

<Callout tone="answer">Say the answer plainly here. The rest of the post shows how I got to it.</Callout>

## What problem I had

Start with the situation, not the solution. What was I building, what broke or got in the way, and what did it cost me in time, money or confidence?

Be concrete. "Deploys took 14 minutes and failed one run in five" beats "deploys were slow and flaky".

## What I tried

List the options in the order I actually tried them. A sentence or two each is enough here; the details belong in the next two sections.

1. The first thing I reached for, and why it seemed right.
2. The second attempt, and what made me move on.
3. The approach I ended up keeping.

## What worked

The approach I kept, and the specific reason it beat the others. Name the trade-off I accepted to get it.

### A sub-step, if it needs one

Use `###` for steps inside a section. Make these headings descriptive ("Why the slug must match the file"), because they show up in the "On this page" rail.

## What failed

The dead ends, and what each one taught me. Readers often learn more here than anywhere else, so don't sand it down.

<Callout tone="warning">If a failure involved leaked data, a security hole or someone else's system, redact first and follow responsible disclosure.</Callout>
