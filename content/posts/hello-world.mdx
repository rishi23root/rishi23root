This is the first post on rishi23root.in. It's where I'll write about the things I build, in more room than a GitHub profile allows.

## TL;DR

<Callout tone="answer">Short, practical notes on SaaS work, automation and security, written when I have something worth sharing. No schedule, no newsletter.</Callout>

## What problem I had

My [GitHub profile README](https://github.com/rishi23root) was doing two jobs: saying who I am, and holding anything I wanted to explain. It's good at the first. It's a poor place to walk through a bug, a design decision or a tool I built, because there's nowhere to put more than a paragraph.

So the writing lives here now, and the README stays short. Both are built from the same folder of content, so the bio and the list of posts can't drift apart. If you want the details, [How this site is built](/writing/how-this-site-works/) covers all of it.

## What to expect

- **Building SaaS products:** the decisions behind features, and the ones I'd undo.
- **Automation:** scripts and pipelines that remove busywork, with the code.
- **Security habits:** the small defaults that keep a project honest without slowing it down.

Posts show up when there's something real to say: a problem, what I tried, and what actually worked. If that's useful to you, there's an [RSS feed](/rss.xml).
